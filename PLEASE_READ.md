# Why I chose it
I chose this for one reason and one reason only. This is for the chip. Why, you may ask? It is because the RK3328 chip has what is called a hardware-accelerated decoder. Now the reason this is important for me is because I will be connecting it to my Batocera Linux PC (if you want to see it) that is hooked up to my TV. Now this means I will be using Jellyfin on the NAS to hold the shows, anime, movies, etc and Kodi Entertainment to play and watch. But if the files are different, I will have to do what is called transcoding, which is if I have to change media from one file type to another. Now, usually for most mini PCs, this is extremely difficult. But since this chip has the hardware-accelerated decoder, it can do it on the hardware and not stress the CPU out. Now since i will be watching on Kodi this will not matter because it will be using direct play but in the case I want to watch on my phone or off the NAS etc transcoding may be necessary and the chip will likely help out.

# Reason 2
Its the cheapest single board computer I could find with 4gb of ram in this economy 😭 Sadly due to to high ram prices 65 dollars is the best I could find.


# Why I would not use it
Under the case you do not want to use Jellyfin and the something like a orange pi is cheaper don't chose it. BUT I would still chose it as of writing this even without Jellyfin do to the better processor and cheaper price that the orange pi.
